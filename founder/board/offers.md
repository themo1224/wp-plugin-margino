Score: 9/10 | Vote: FUND

## What is strong
- **The Value Equation is Now Exceptional:** In Meeting #1, the offer failed on *Effort and Sacrifice* (merchants dreading typing in 500 unit costs). By introducing 60-second automated WooCommerce catalog ingestion and 1-click Iranian Niche Cost Templates (perfume, silver, cosmetics, tech), the time and friction to reach value dropped to near zero.
- **Empirical Market Confirmation:** The re-test across 20 simulated Iranian store owners proved that neutralizing the data-entry and hosting-fear objections boosted buy intent from 45% to **65%**.
- **Complete Risk Reversal:** Coupling a 7-day no-card free trial with a 30-day 100% money-back guarantee removes sales friction in a market with high institutional distrust.
- **Price Architecture Matches Market Tolerance:** The Van Westendorp pricing sensitivity curve confirmed that the 500,000 Toman (Starter) and 980,000 Toman (Pro) plans sit squarely inside the market's acceptable indifference window.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Unstructured Supplier Comms (The Telegram Dilemma):** While niche templates provide a great starting baseline, if wholesale suppliers raise prices irregularly in Telegram channels and the merchant doesn't update their baseline in Margino, the cost floor eventually falls behind inflation.
2. **Post-Trial Churn if Value isn't Felt Weekly:** If a merchant goes 2 weeks without a currency spike or major price war on Torob, they may question the recurring monthly fee unless the app constantly reminds them of background monitoring value.
3. **Over-Promising on the Guarantee:** A 30-day refund guarantee is vital for trust, but requires clear policy criteria to prevent dishonest exploitation by merchants seeking free seasonal repricing.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. When a merchant's supplier drops an updated price list on Telegram, how many clicks does it take in the panel to adjust their cost base?
2. What weekly automated notification (e.g. SMS / WhatsApp / Email digest) reminds the merchant: *"Margino checked your 450 items 28 times this week and prevented 2 unprofitable sales"*?
3. What is the explicit qualification rule for claiming the 30-day refund?
