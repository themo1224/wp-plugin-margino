These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- A heavily discounted annual plan or lifetime license.
- A direct Telegram price-list parser or helper.
- Video walkthroughs and testimonials from recognizable store owners.
- A mini tier for under 100 products at 200,000 Toman/mo.
- A micro tier at 190,000 Toman/mo.
- Real Iranian store case studies and before/after sales stats.
- A direct gold/USD price multiplier feed for cost baselines.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
